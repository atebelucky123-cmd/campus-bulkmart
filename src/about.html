Our Story

From a simple idea to LASUCOM's most trusted campus delivery platform — here's how Campus Bulkmart was born.

The Origin

We Solved Our Own Problem

Every LASUCOM student knows the struggle — you're mid-assignment, your data runs out, your toiletries are empty, and the market feels a world away. Campus Bulkmart was born from exactly that frustration.

We built a digital procurement platform that acts as your personal campus runner — you browse, you order, we go to the market and deliver straight to your hostel door. No hidden fees, no long waits, no stress.

What started as a simple idea has grown into a full-service campus superstore serving students across all LASUCOM hostels daily.
